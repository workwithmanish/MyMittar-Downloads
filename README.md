## 📲 How to Install the APK on Android

1. Tap [📥 **Download Official APK**](https://github.com/workwithmanish/MyMittar-Downloads/releases/latest) above to download the `.apk` package.
2. When the download completes, tap the download notification or open your device's **Downloads** folder.
3. If Android prompts *"Install unknown apps"*:
   - Tap **Settings**.
   - Enable **"Allow from this source"** (or *"Install unknown apps"* for your browser/file manager).
4. Tap **Install**.
5. Launch **MyMittar**, complete your quick profile setup & video verification, and start connecting!
